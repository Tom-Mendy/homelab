# Third-party notices

This repository configures and, in a few cases, vendors material from projects
under their own licenses. The Apache-2.0 license in this repository applies to
the original configuration and documentation only.

Before redistributing a vendored chart, dashboard, image, or generated asset,
check the upstream license and retain its required notices. Current items to
review include:

- the vendored Open WebUI Helm chart archive under
  `kubernetes/ollama/charts/`;
- Grafana dashboards under `kubernetes/grafana/dashboards/`;
- container images and Helm charts referenced by the Flux configuration.

Upstream projects keep ownership of their code, names, trademarks, and license
terms.
